Methodology

This tool does not use complex machine learning. The Dependency Level is calculated transparently using three factors: Brunson's scoring average over his last 10 games, the historical difficulty of the opponent, and home/away splits. These factors determine the likely scoring band, which is then mapped against historical Knicks win rates to assess how desperately a big night is needed.
